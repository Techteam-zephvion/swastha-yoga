# IIYN Yogic Health — Website Reference

**URL:** https://iiynyogichealth.com

## Summary

IIYN Yogic Health is the website for the **Indian Institute of Yoga & Naturopathy (IIYN)**, an organization offering yoga therapy and drugless, naturopathic treatments. The site positions the institute as a provider of "100% Drugless Solutions" for transforming body, mind, and soul through yoga therapy.

## About

- Founded in **2004** with the mission of uplifting human values through Indian culture and traditional medicine.
- Offers yoga therapy, nature cure, traditional rejuvenation programs, and drugless treatment systems.
- Runs regular fitness programs, youth education on preventive health/personality development, and women's wellness programs.
- Has organized social, spiritual, ethical, moral, and health-related programs across the Mysore district and Bangalore.
- Recently expanded into **IIYN Yogic Health Pvt Ltd**, aiming to scale services across Karnataka, then nationally, with an eventual goal of becoming a "Global Foundation" to spread traditional Indian health practices worldwide.
